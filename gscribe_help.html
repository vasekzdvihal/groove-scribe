Help

Groove Scribe is a drum notation editor. You tap notes into a grid. The page writes the sheet music and plays it.

Add notes

  • Tap a cell in the grid to turn a note on. Tap it again to turn it off.
  • The sheet music above the grid changes at once.
  • On a touch screen, use Advanced in the toolbar to choose a note type, such as accent, ghost or flam. Then a tap on a cell opens a menu of note types.
  • Use Toms to show the tom rows.
  • Stickings opens a menu. It can show or hide the stickings row, or reverse R and L.
  • The time and the note division are the first two buttons in the toolbar.
  • Undo and Redo step through your changes.
  • Grooves in the toolbar has ready-made grooves to start from.

Play

  • Tap the round play button at the bottom left. Tap it again to stop.
  • Use − and + next to BPM to change the tempo.
  • On most browsers, the screen stays on while the groove plays. It stays on for 10 minutes after you stop.

Save and share

  • Share gives you a link to the groove. Send the link to anyone. They see the same groove in their browser.
  • Save is next to Share. It keeps the groove in your library. You must sign in with Google to use it.
  • When you open a saved groove and change it, Save offers Update groove. This replaces the saved groove. Save as new makes a copy instead.
  • The button shows Saved when the groove in the editor has no unsaved changes.
  • The More actions button (three dots) has Print, Download and Clear all notes.

Library

  • Tap Library at the top left to open the library.
  • Sign in with Google. Then your lessons and grooves are on every device that you sign in on.
  • A lesson holds grooves. You can rename and delete lessons and grooves. You can move a groove to another lesson.
  • Tap a groove to open it in the editor.
  • If the groove in the editor has unsaved changes, the page asks first. Choose Keep editing to stay on your groove. Choose Discard changes to open the other groove.
  • On a wide screen the library stays open next to the music. On a narrow screen it slides over the editor.
  • Without a sign-in the editor still works. Only the library needs it.

Offline and install

  • Open Groove Scribe once with a network. After that, the editor works without a network.
  • Your saved library is on this device if you opened it signed in before. Changes you make offline are sent when the network comes back.
  • You can add Groove Scribe to your home screen. On an iPad, tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. In Chrome, use the install option in the menu.
  • The home screen icon opens the app in full screen.

Metronome

Use the Click buttons in the play bar: Off, 4th, 8th or 16th.

  • 4th puts a cowbell on the 1 and a click on the 2, 3 and 4.
  • 8th puts a click on all the "ands". In triplets it plays 8th note triplets.
  • 16th puts a soft click on every 16th note and a louder click on the 2, 3 and 4. In triplets it plays 16th note triplets.

The cog button next to them has more options.

  • Solo plays only the metronome. The groove is quiet.
  • Auto speed up raises the tempo after each loop.
  • Count it in plays one measure of clicks before the groove starts.
  • Offset click puts the accent on the "e", the "&" or the "a" instead of the 1.

Swing

The Swing button in the play bar opens a slider. It changes the sound only. The sheet music stays the same.

There is no right amount of swing. Try different amounts. At 33% you hear the classic triplet feel. At 50% you hear a tight feel: a dotted 8th note and a 16th note. Wikipedia has an article on swing.

20%3:2light
